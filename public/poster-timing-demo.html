What the problem was (simple)

Posters use Iqamah (Jamaat) time — the right-hand column on the timetable — not Beginning/Adhan.

If you set 20 minutes before prayer, the poster should start at:

Jamaat time − 20 minutes

The built-in Adhan countdown also starts exactly 20 minutes before Jamaat (for 30 seconds). So at that moment you see the countdown first; the poster appears right after those 30 seconds — still inside the poster window.
